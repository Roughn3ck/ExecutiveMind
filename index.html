The firm

Executive Mind was founded by Kris Racette. Kris built a Gold Coast property group into four businesses — property management, commercial and residential sales, holiday rentals and business brokering — before the group was acquired by Ray White. An accountant by training (MCom, CPA track), he now builds bespoke digital infrastructure for Brisbane businesses: the same operator's discipline, applied to web, AI operations, process optimisation and security.

The firm is deliberately small. A principal, a senior workflow, and a working pack of specialised AI agents doing the heavy lifting. No juniors on your budget, no layers between you and the work.

You deal with one person. That person ships.
